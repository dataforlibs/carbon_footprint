Dates as YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM. A line with a comma, tab or semicolon is read as date,count. Publications before the first window count in it; those after the last are ignored. Nothing leaves your browser.

To compare lifepaths, add them under the same settings. Every lifepath on this page is fitted on the same grid of windows, so they can be drawn together. If you change the period or frequency, the lifepaths already added are cleared, because they would no longer be comparable.

How it works

For each half-yearly snapshot the cumulative publications are scaled to 1, padded with zero windows before the first one (135 minus the snapshot number, as in the paper), and a three-parameter sigmoid is fitted. Each fit gives an inflection time x₀, a steepness k and a ceiling A. The lifepath point is x₀, converted to a date, against the log of k. Phases follow the point files of the paper: Deactivated if the fitted ceiling is less than 5% above the current total; otherwise Inflection if x₀ lies within 30 days of the snapshot date, Acceleration if it lies later, and Deceleration if it lies earlier.

Folds are backward moves of x₀ of more than 183 days between consecutive snapshots (see Time folds). A fold is also marked post-convergence when x₀ is within the observation window (not beyond 1) in both snapshots.

The fit is done by a small optimiser in the browser, not by the same library as the paper. On the paper's 114 countries it reproduces the phases and dates closely, but early snapshots with very few publications are numerically fragile, and a date can differ by a few weeks. For published results use the scripts in the package.
